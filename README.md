### About

I am an undergraduate in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Dhaka. My work centres on **machine learning and computer vision**, with a particular interest in rigorous evaluation: building models that are measured honestly against strong baselines. I also build **full-stack web applications** and have a foundation in **systems programming**.

My undergraduate thesis develops a **single-camera, depth-aware vision-language navigation assistant for blind and low-vision users**. It is designed for the unstructured streets of Bangladesh and is trained in part on a new dataset that our team recorded in Dhaka.

### Research

**Vision-Language Generative Models for Assistive Navigation for the Blind** *(undergraduate thesis, in progress)*
- A lightweight encoder-decoder CNN for monocular depth estimation achieved **49% lower MSE** than the Depth Anything V2 foundation model on the SANPO outdoor pedestrian dataset.
- YOLOv8 obstacle detection runs at **113 FPS**, with distance-based collision warnings and spoken guidance.
- Original Dhaka street-navigation dataset: about 60 recorded videos and 18 object classes, including rickshaws, CNGs and broken road surfaces.
